ABOUT ELARA

Turning compassion into action.

Founded in 2023, ELARA Women Foundation exists to advance the dignity, safety and potential of women, girls and vulnerable members of our communities.

We believe meaningful change begins when communities are equipped with knowledge, opportunities, protection and a voice in decisions affecting their lives.

Our Mission

To empower women and girls, protect human rights, prevent violence and harmful practices, and support stronger and more inclusive communities.

Our Vision

A society where every woman and girl can live safely, exercise her rights and access meaningful opportunities without discrimination or violence.

OUR AGENDA

Women, Girls, Human Rights & Community Empowerment.

Our agenda focuses on empowering women and girls, protecting human rights, preventing FGM and gender-based violence, supporting education and mental health, strengthening child protection, and advancing inclusive community development across Kenya.
